Right Livelihood as Right Lifestyle

Bhante Bodhidhamma 16:22 Dharma Talks
Source: satipanya.org.uk

In this Dharma talk celebrating the November full moon, Bhante Bodhidhamma examines sammā ājīva (right livelihood) as one of the components of the Noble Eightfold Path. He begins by outlining the Buddha's traditional warnings about five types of businesses to avoid: weapons, trading in people, meat, intoxicants, and poison, while acknowledging the complexities of applying these ancient guidelines in modern contexts.

Drawing from the Upadasutta and the Buddha's teaching to Ānanda that good spiritual friendship constitutes the whole of the spiritual life, Bhante extends the concept of right livelihood to encompass our entire lifestyle. He suggests viewing all our activities—work, leisure, relationships—through the lens of how they support or hinder our spiritual development. Using personal examples, he illustrates how Dharma practice naturally causes us to seek balance in our lives and how our interests may evolve as we center our lives around spiritual practice.

This practical approach encourages practitioners to examine whether their complete lifestyle—not just their job—aligns with and supports their spiritual path, creating coherence between all aspects of life and Dharma practice.
